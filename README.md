## 🔍 Project Overview
Managing an e-commerce platform efficiently requires tracking sales trends, order statuses, customer purchasing behavior, and inventory movement. This project implements fundamental data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and descriptive statistics in Microsoft Excel to answer key business questions:
* *Which product categories generate the highest revenue and sales volume?*
* *How do order statuses (Shipped, Delivered, Returned, Cancelled) impact overall fulfillment?*
* *What are the preferred payment methods and customer purchasing patterns across channels?*

## 🚨 Business Problems Identified
1. **High Return and Cancellation Rates**: A significant portion of orders experience cancellations (~250 orders) and returns (~247 orders), indicating potential fulfillment or product satisfaction issues.
2. **Evenly Distributed Payment Preferences**: Payment channels (Online, Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card, Gift Card) are heavily fragmented, requiring tailored gateway optimization.
3. **Product Inventory Balance**: Certain electronics and furniture items (Printers, Tablets, Laptops) drive heavy volume, requiring robust stock management to prevent stockouts.

## 💡 Strategic Business Recommendations

1. **Reduce Order Cancellations & Returns**: 
   * Investigate root causes behind returned and cancelled items by analyzing product quality descriptions and shipping timelines.
2. **Optimize Inventory for Top Products**: 
   * Prioritize stock replenishment for high-demand items like Printers and Tablets to capture maximum revenue during peak shopping cycles.
3. **Streamline Payment Gateway Fees**: 
   * Encourage lower-cost digital transaction channels over cash or high-fee payment gateways to improve transaction margins.
4. **Enhance Customer Retention**: 
   * Target repeat buyers from top referral channels with personalized discount coupons (e.g., `SAVE10`) to drive customer lifetime value.
